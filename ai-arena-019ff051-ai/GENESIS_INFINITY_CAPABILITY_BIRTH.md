## 1. Multi-Artifact Capability Genesis

Capability Genesis creates six distinct artifact types:
- `tool`: Deterministic Python execution cell.
- `skill`: Reusable algorithmic logic.
- `procedure`: Condition-action sequence.
- `strategy`: High-level cognitive method of thinking.
- `cognitive_strategy`: Composed reasoning & verification topology.
- `learning_strategy`: Adaptive curriculum generation routine.

---

## 2. Generalization & Negative Testing Boundary

Newly synthesized capabilities are validated against:
1. Seen training task ($\ge 95\%$ accuracy).
2. Structurally similar unseen task ($\ge 90\%$ accuracy).
3. Negative tests with malformed/non-dict inputs (must reject gracefully).
4. Empty inputs and boundary cases (must handle safely).
