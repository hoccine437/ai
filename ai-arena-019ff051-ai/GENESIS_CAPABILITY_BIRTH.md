## 1. Multi-Artifact Capability Genesis

Capability Birth 3.0 creates six distinct artifact types:
- **`tool`**: Deterministic Python sandbox function.
- **`skill`**: Reusable algorithmic transformation.
- **`procedure`**: Condition-action sequence.
- **`strategy`**: Multi-step cognitive problem-solving approach.
- **`cognitive_strategy`**: Composed reasoning and verification topology.
- **`learning_strategy`**: Adaptive curriculum generation routine.

---

## 2. Multi-Variant Generalization Validation

A newly born capability is never trusted based solely on its training task. It must pass:
1. **Seen Task:** Original failure context ($\ge 95\%$ accuracy).
2. **Structurally Similar Unseen Task:** Variant with different data sizes ($\ge 90\%$ accuracy).
3. **Domain-Shifted Task:** Cross-domain parameterization ($\ge 85\%$ accuracy).
4. **Adversarial Stress Variant:** Malformed, boundary, and extreme inputs ($\ge 85\%$ accuracy).
