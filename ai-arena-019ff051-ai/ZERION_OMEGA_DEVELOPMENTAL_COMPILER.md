## 1. Candidate Architecture Evolution

```
[BOTTLENECK IDENTIFIED] ──► [SYNTHESIZE PROPOSAL] ──► [SANDBOX TEST] ──► [CANARY PROMOTION]
```

Every proposal requires an isolated sandbox verification harness (`sandbox_test_code`) before promotion to the live organism.
