## 1. Attention Allocation & Decision Hierarchy

The system computes priority for every perceived signal:

$$\text{Attention Priority} = \frac{\text{Urgency} \times \text{Importance} \times \text{Uncertainty} \times \text{Novelty} \times \text{Objective Relevance} \times \text{EIG}}{\max(0.1, \text{Resource Cost})} \times 1000$$

### Attention Operational Decisions:
- `IGNORE`: Sub-threshold ambient noise.
- `MONITOR`: Low-urgency drift tracked in background.
- `REFLEX`: High-certainty, low-risk procedural execution.
- `THINK`: Single-pass cognitive program synthesis.
- `DEEP_THINK`: Multi-path reasoning with adversarial verification.
- `EXPERIMENT`: Sandbox reality testing for high-uncertainty hypotheses.
- `LEARN`: Experience distillation and procedural compression.
