## 1. Epistemic Void Modeling

The `UnknownSpaceEngine` explicitly models epistemic frontiers and blind spots:
- `KNOWN`: Empirically verified beliefs.
- `KNOWN_UNKNOWN`: Identified gaps in the knowledge graph (e.g. unobserved properties).
- `UNKNOWN_UNKNOWN_CANDIDATE`: Latent unobserved third variables suspected during causal inference.
- `CONTRADICTION`: Conflicting empirical observations flagged for immediate investigation.
- `BLIND_SPOT`: Entities or state spaces with low sampling frequency.

---

## 2. Priority Ranking of Unknowns

$$\text{Investigation Priority} = \min(1.0, \text{Entropy Score} \times 1.2)$$

High-entropy voids are automatically transformed into Question Genesis targets during each flywheel cycle.
