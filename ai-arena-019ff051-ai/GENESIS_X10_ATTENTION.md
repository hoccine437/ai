## 1. The Attention Allocation Formula

To prevent cognitive overload, GENESIS ×10 establishes an Attention Economy:

$$\text{Attention Priority} = \frac{\text{Urgency} \times \text{Importance} \times \text{Uncertainty} \times \text{Novelty} \times \text{Objective Relevance} \times \text{Expected Information Gain}}{\max(0.1, \text{Resource Cost})} \times 1000$$

### Key Operating Principle:
$$\mathbf{PERCEPTION \ne COGNITION}$$
- **Perception:** Broad, lightweight, continuous ingestion of all environmental signals.
- **Cognition:** Selective, deep, high-value processing allocated strictly to the top items in the Attention Pool.
